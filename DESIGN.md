## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the high-caliber engineering persona of Hemavathi M, an AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning CSE engineer. The visual identity bridges deep computational rigor with bleeding-edge innovation, evoking the aura of advanced neural networks, high-throughput compute, and algorithmic clarity.

The target audience comprises Tier-1 tech recruiters, engineering managers, AI lab leads, venture investors, and research collaborators. The UI conveys authoritative technical competence, mathematical elegance, and forward-looking research leadership.

The aesthetic philosophy is an exact convergence of **Refined Futurism** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Deep void canvas surfaces (`#0A0A0F`) anchored by subtle structural gridlines and aurora gradient bleeds.
- Controlled atmospheric lighting mimicking neural network synaptic pulses, luminous tensor computations, and laser-precise optical lenses.
- Strict visual discipline: glows and chromatic aberrations serve as navigational cues, technical state indicators, and focus anchors rather than superficial decoration.

## Colors

The color palette captures an atmospheric computational space where deep quantum black creates infinite contrast against ionized photonic accents.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#8B5CF6` - Electric Violet):** Represents neural depth, synthetic cognition, and executive technical status. Used for core interactive states, focal highlights, and dynamic gradient stops.
- **Secondary (`#EC4899` - Radiant Fuchsia):** Denotes hyperparameter tuning, model iteration, and high-impact accomplishments. Serves as secondary gradient blends and key CTA accents.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Vivid Cyan):** Reflects high-velocity inference, real-time data streaming, and metrics. Used for live indicators, code badges, precision metrics, and latency stats.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0A0A0F` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational substrate that absorbs light and yields maximum contrast for illuminated overlays.

### Surface Tiers & Translucency
- **Canvas Base:** `#0A0A0F` (Solid)
- **Glass Surface Level 1 (Default Card):** `rgba(18, 18, 29, 0.65)` with backdrop-filter blur `16px`
- **Glass Surface Level 2 (Elevated / Hover):** `rgba(26, 26, 42, 0.75)` with backdrop-filter blur `24px`
- **Subtle Rim Border (Standard):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Illuminated Rim Border (Interactive / Focused):** Dynamic linear-gradient at 135deg across `#8B5CF6`, `#EC4899`, and `#06B6D4` with `0.4` peak opacity.

### Typography & Content Shades
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F8FAFC` (Purest foreground, headings, focal points)
- **Text Muted / Body:** `#94A3B8` (Legible body copy, longform descriptions)
- **Text Subtle / Metadata:** `#64748B` (Timestamps, secondary mono parameters)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural clarity by pairing three specialized typefaces:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Structural Display):** Provides architectural confidence, geometric stability, and an uncompromising modern aesthetic without becoming overly decorative. Used for hero titles, section divisions, and case study headlines.
2. **Inter (Body & Narrative Prose):** Delivers clean readability for detailed project narratives, architectural breakdowns, research publications, and resume timelines.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Badges, Metrics, Code, and System Metadata):** Delivers an authentic developer terminal cadence. Used for benchmark metrics, model parameters (e.g., `FLOPs`, `Val Loss`, `epoch: 120`), latency figures, and architecture tags.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Caps Labels:** All `label-caps` must use uppercase transformations (`text-transform: uppercase`) paired with positive tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`).
- **Numbers & Data:** Any quantitative evaluation (accuracy %, latency in ms, GPU throughput) must strictly render in `JetBrains Mono` with tabular numerals enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Gradient Text:** Hero headings may apply gradient text masking (`background-clip: text`) transitioning smoothly from `#F8FAFC` through `#8B5CF6` to `#EC4899`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a fluid, modular 12-column grid system bounded inside a maximum content width of `1280px` (`80rem`). This creates generous negative space that reinforces the deep space aesthetic.

### Breakpoints & Column Distributions
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` column gutters. Components stack into single-column ribbons.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Project cards and metric clusters arrange into 2-column matrices.
- **Desktop (≥ 1025px):** 12-column layout with `4rem` outer margins and `2rem` gutters. Accommodates side-by-side terminal demos, interactive neural architecture diagrams, and asymmetric narrative showcases.

### Layout Rhythm
- Keep vertical cadence anchored to multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- Major page sections are partitioned with deep breathing space (`5rem` to `8rem` padding), occasionally bisected by ultra-thin hairline borders with central glow gradients (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through frosted glass layered over cosmic glow origins, rather than opaque skeuomorphic shadows. The canvas feels like a dark optical chamber with backlit crystalline plates.

### Atmosphere & Aurora Fields
- Ambient background glows are produced using radial gradient blobs placed strategically behind core cards and sections:
  - Cyan Aurora: `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 20%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12) 0%, transparent 60%)`
  - Violet/Fuchsia Synapse: `radial-gradient(circle at 90% 80%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15) 0%, rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.08) 50%, transparent 70%)`

### Layering Levels
1. **Level 0 (Base Grid):** Pure `#0A0A0F` featuring an ultra-subtle 32px CSS isometric dot grid (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).
2. **Level 1 (Ambient Glass Surface):** 
   - Background: `rgba(18, 18, 29, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop-filter: `blur(16px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`
   - Box-shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Card / Active Modal):**
   - Background: `rgba(26, 26, 42, 0.8)`
   - Backdrop-filter: `blur(24px)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`
   - Box-shadow: `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`
4. **Level 3 (Focused State / Active Glow):**
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`
   - Box-shadow: `0 0 32px 0 rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25), inset 0 0 16px 0 rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design uses a refined, moderately rounded shape aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`). This choice blends software sophistication with geometric precision, steering clear of toy-like circular elements while avoiding harsh, dated industrial edges.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard Cards & Containers:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` radius (`rounded-lg`), providing balanced containment for glass surfaces and inner glow borders.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`), delivering a tactile, responsive feel.
- **Badges, Status Tags, and Chips:** `0.375rem` (`rounded-sm`), retaining sharp, technical, data-sheet precision.
- **Avatars & Optical Pulsers:** Strict circular silhouettes (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for live state beacons and profile frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Aurora Surge):** Gradient fill transitioning 135deg from `#8B5CF6` to `#EC4899`. White bold text, high-contrast, padded `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover triggers a diffused drop glow: `0 0 24px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.45)` with a `scale(1.02)` transform.
- **Secondary (Glass Wireframe):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: border tints to `#06B6D4` with text color transitioning to `#06B6D4`.
- **Tertiary (Code Ghost):** Pure transparent background, `JetBrains Mono` font, text `#94A3B8`, underlined on hover with electric violet accent.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Model Chips:** Backing `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`, text `#06B6D4`, rendered in `code-badge` typography. Used for tags like `PyTorch`, `Transformer`, `CUDA`, `LLaMA-3`.
- **Status Indicator:** Features an animated ping dot: a 6px circle `#06B6D4` with an outer pulsing ring indicating model serving availability or interview readiness.

### Cards (Project, Publication & Experience)
- **Structure:** Level 1 Glass Surface with internal padding `1.75rem`.
- **Interactive Hover:** Translates `translateY(-4px)` with a progressive transition (`duration-300 ease-out`). Border transitions dynamically from faint white to a dual-color violet/cyan gradient line.
- **Inner Header:** Houses category tag in `JetBrains Mono` on the left and metric/year on the right.

### Input Fields & Terminal Simulators
- **Input Fields:** Dark tinted fill `rgba(10, 10, 15, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focus state injects a glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.
- **Interactive Terminal / Code Viewer:** Top chrome with three muted dots (`#EF4444`, `#F59E0B`, `#10B981` at 50% opacity). Dark translucent canvas `#08080C` showcasing executable Python/PyTorch inference snippets with glowing syntax highlights.

### Metrics & Benchmark Displays
- **Key-Value Pair Tiles:** Used for displaying engineering impact (e.g., `99.2% Top-1 Acc`, `<12ms P99 Latency`, `4.2M Params`). Value renders in `JetBrains Mono` with text gradient from `#F8FAFC` to `#06B6D4`. Accompanying label displays beneath in `label-caps`.
